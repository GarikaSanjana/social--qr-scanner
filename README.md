# 🌐 Social QR

A simple and elegant web project that displays all your social media accounts and generates a **QR code** to share them instantly.  
Anyone who scans your QR can open your hosted profile page and access your socials in one place.

## 🧠 Features

✅ Displays all your social media accounts  
✅ Generates a shareable QR code  
✅ Lets users download the QR as a PNG  
✅ Responsive and mobile-friendly  
✅ Includes a QR scanner page that decodes links or embedded account data
